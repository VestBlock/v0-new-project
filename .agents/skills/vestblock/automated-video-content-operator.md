# Automated Video Content Operator

Use this skill when planning, generating, reviewing, publishing, repurposing, or learning from VestBlock video content.

## Goal

Turn one verified buyer problem into a brand-safe video, approved distribution assets, and a durable learning record without creating a second content system.

## Start here

Read in this order:

1. `docs/VESTBLOCK_IMMUTABLE_BRAND_DECISIONS.md`
2. `docs/content/VESTBLOCK_CONTENT_COMMAND_CENTER.md`
3. `docs/content/VESTBLOCK_AUTOMATED_VIDEO_CONTENT_SYSTEM.md`
4. `docs/content/video/VESTBLOCK_VIDEO_PILOT_001_REVIEW.md` when operating the first pilot
5. `.agents/skills/vestblock/compliance-safe-credit-content.md` for credit, funding, grants, or financial topics
6. the current brief, source packet, and latest approved video learning

Use `content_assets`, the existing improvement tables, Supabase, and the Command Center. Do not introduce a second dashboard, CRM, scheduler, content database, or learning store.

## Operating loop

1. Read current performance and customer-question signals.
2. Choose one audience, one pain, one next move, and one hypothesis.
3. Collect sources and write claim limitations before the script.
4. Produce the script for the ear: hook, context, example, takeaway, CTA.
5. Review brand, facts, financial claims, privacy, rights, and synthetic-media disclosure.
6. Create the storyboard and renderer-specific prompt.
7. Render a private draft.
8. Verify duration, frame, audio, captions, links, metadata, visuals, facts, rights, and disclosure.
9. Get approval before public publishing.
10. Capture 24-hour, 7-day, and 28-day performance evidence.
11. Propose one learning with confidence and scope.
12. Apply only approved learning to `prompt_versions`, `strategy_updates`, or the next brief.

## Renderer routing

- HeyGen presenter or hybrid: use `heygen-video`.
- New persistent presenter identity: run `heygen-avatar` first and stop at appearance and voice approval gates.
- Drawn explanation: try HeyGen Blueprint, Marker & Sharpie, Chalkboard, Journal, or Post-Its before adding another renderer.
- Hyper-real scenario: use HeyGen AI/stock scenes or approved generated assets; mark realistic synthetic-media disclosure correctly.
- Screen demo: use authentic VestBlock screens and keep customer data out of the recording.
- Exact deterministic motion: use a specialist such as Remotion or HyperFrames only after a measured need is approved.

## Guardrails

- Preserve Capital + Deals + Opportunity and “Find your next move.”
- Do not narrow VestBlock to one product, service, or vertical.
- Do not guarantee funding, approvals, limits, rates, rankings, citations, score changes, deletions, grants, offers, matches, closings, revenue, or timelines.
- Do not fabricate customers, testimonials, quotes, results, screenshots, documents, or events.
- Do not present hyper-real synthetic scenes as real footage.
- Do not imitate reference creators' identity, wording, voice, characters, music, footage, or distinctive visual assets.
- Keep private documents, lead details, contact information, credentials, and customer data out of prompts and generated media.
- Upload private or unlisted during the pilot. Public publishing requires approval.
- Never learn from simulated analytics.

## Self-improvement contract

- Compare the same format, language, pillar, traffic source, and age window.
- Two observations create a low-confidence idea only.
- Five comparable assets or a native concurrent experiment are required to change a default.
- Require the pattern to repeat across two measurement windows.
- Store hypothesis and result in `experiment_results`.
- Store evidence-backed recommendations in `improvement_insights`.
- Store approved prompt changes in `prompt_versions`.
- Queue claim, CTA, offer, avatar, and public-strategy changes in `strategy_updates` for review.
- Record the next bottleneck after every cycle.

## Verification

- Sources resolve and support the exact claims.
- Script passes the four-question Content Standard.
- No prohibited guarantee or fake proof remains.
- Video and captions are readable in the target orientation.
- Audio is present, intelligible, and appropriately licensed.
- CTA URL works and includes the intended attribution parameters.
- Realistic AI disclosure is set when required.
- Publication begins private/unlisted and the final state is logged.
- Performance snapshots and any approved learning are written to durable storage.

## Output

Report:

1. asset and audience;
2. hypothesis and format;
3. source and compliance status;
4. render and approval status;
5. publication and measurement status;
6. learning captured;
7. next bottleneck.
