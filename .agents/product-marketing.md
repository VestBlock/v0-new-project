# VestBlock product and marketing context

## Company

- Legal business name: Vestblock LLC.
- Brand name: VestBlock.
- Website: `https://vestblock.io`.
- Positioning: a real-estate opportunity routing and operating platform connecting capital, deals, and the people needed to move them forward.
- Brand promise: Find your next move.

## Core product lanes

1. Capital — organize borrower and deal context, then route qualified files toward relevant funding-review paths.
2. Deals — collect seller opportunities, buyer criteria, property context, and partner capacity so the right conversations can be matched.
3. Opportunity — help buyers, lenders, contractors, developers, operators, and service partners become easier to discover and understand.

DealVault is the trust and proof layer for referral sources, agreements, milestones, documents, partner roles, and payout records. VestBlock's founder cockpit is the operating layer: Today, Pipeline, Growth, AI Brain, and System.

## Audiences

- Property sellers who need a realistic next-step review.
- Real-estate buyers and investors with defined buy boxes.
- Business owners and real-estate operators seeking capital options.
- Lenders and capital partners with defined lending boxes.
- Contractors, developers, wholesalers, and service partners.
- Referral and operating partners who need cleaner proof and accountability.

## Differentiators

- One intake and routing layer across capital, deals, and opportunity.
- Better-fit matching based on criteria, context, readiness, and constraints.
- DealVault proof and partner-accountability records.
- AI-assisted operations with founder approval for high-risk decisions.
- Strategy-to-campaign-to-lead-to-revenue attribution.

## Required claim guardrails

- Never promise or imply guaranteed funding, loan approval, offers, rates, rankings, closings, returns, savings, deal volume, or timelines.
- Never invent customers, testimonials, performance metrics, licenses, partnerships, or transaction history.
- State that terms and eligibility are subject to underwriting or partner review when discussing capital.
- Do not describe VestBlock as a lender, mortgage broker, real-estate broker, closing agent, law firm, credit-repair organization, or investment adviser unless current legal authority and approved copy explicitly support the claim.
- Avoid credit-repair, debt-settlement, or deceptive financial-outcome messaging.
- Keep advertising about the VestBlock platform or an educational/service journey; do not use ChatGPT Ads to promote an individual home or rental listing.
- Treat financial-services advertising as restricted and subject to OpenAI approval. Do not launch, fund, or materially change an ad campaign without founder approval.
