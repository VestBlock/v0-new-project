# VestBlock opportunity film direction

## Purpose

Replace the abstract network treatment on the public home page with a readable, cinematic account of how a real-estate opportunity becomes ready to move. This is a corrective pass within the approved VestBlock platform, not a rebrand and not a change to application behavior.

## Narrative

**Opening — property intelligence.** A moving, architectural city frame establishes a physical environment before any language about capital appears. The camera is the focal motion. A calm, dark grade protects the copy.

**Middle — capital diligence.** The city view gives way to a restrained underwriting brief. This is a working surface, not a dashboard: asset context, requested terms, source materials, and the remaining decision are arranged like a real review file.

**Close — qualified execution.** The record becomes an accountable next action. The work moves to the appropriate owner, buyer, lender, operator, or advisor with the context required to decide and a private DealVault record that persists.

## Visual rules

- Keep the VestBlock mark, black, warm paper, steel, and signal-lime system.
- Treat lime as a single operational accent, never an ambient glow field.
- Use the supplied city film as the hero motion and keep it visible behind a stable editorial composition.
- Use crop, occlusion, and paper-like material changes to connect the three acts.
- Typography is editorial and direct: large reading headline, small supporting copy, no decorative status labels.
- On mobile, retain the film and the narrative order while removing nonessential framing.

## Explicit exclusions

No hexagons, polygons, glowing nodes, particle fields, orbit lines, neon routes, floating technical labels, glass panels, crypto aesthetics, stock 3D objects, or equal feature-card rows. Do not imply precision through made-up financial metrics.

## Motion and performance

- The city film is the only continuous visual loop in the opening.
- GSAP and ScrollTrigger drive the editorial entrance and chapter handoff; Lenis continues to provide scroll continuity.
- `prefers-reduced-motion` preserves every content state while removing scroll transforms and stopping the film.
- The hero always ships with a poster image and a reserved media box. There is no blank or loading-state flash.

## Styleframes represented in the implementation

1. **Desktop opening:** a wide architectural dusk frame, copy held in the left third, a single small narrative index near the lower edge.
2. **Desktop mid-scroll:** the same physical world is occluded by a restrained, high-contrast underwriting brief, with the current decision visible rather than a data visualization.
3. **Mobile:** the city frame is tightly cropped behind the headline; the chapter sequence becomes a vertical reading path with the current decision surface above it.
