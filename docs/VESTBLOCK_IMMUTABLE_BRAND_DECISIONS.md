# VestBlock LLC — Immutable Brand Decisions

Status: **Phase 1 approval document**
Authority: the latest corrective brief, the original VestBlock consolidation brief, and the approved continuation briefs
Implementation state: **not implemented**

## Purpose

This document prevents a focused addition or visual fix from silently changing VestBlock's company position. It governs public copy, navigation, homepage hierarchy, visual direction, SEO metadata, campaign language, and future implementation prompts.

## Source-of-truth order

When two briefs conflict, use this order:

1. The latest explicit user correction: VestBlock must not be narrowed to a real-estate platform, partner network, lender marketplace, DealVault product, software product, or any single vertical.
2. The original consolidation decision: VestBlock is one platform organized around **Capital + Deals + Opportunity**, with **Find your next move** as the brand promise.
3. Approved continuation guardrails: retain graphite, ivory, and electric lime; use the VB identity with purpose; keep DealVault connected to the wider platform; remove customer-facing internal language.
4. Vertical-specific briefs: real-estate, capital, DealVault, growth, and other lane-specific language may guide those individual routes, but may not redefine the master brand or homepage.
5. Current production: useful only as implementation evidence. Current production copy is not brand authority where it conflicts with items 1–4.

## Immutable brand decisions

These decisions may not change without explicit approval from the owner of VestBlock LLC.

### Company and promise

- The legal business name is **VestBlock LLC**.
- The public brand name is **VestBlock**.
- The core promise is **Find your next move.**
- VestBlock is one coordinated platform. It is not a directory of unrelated tools or a collection of separate businesses.

### Master platform structure

- **Capital** is a primary doorway.
- **Deals** is a primary doorway.
- **Opportunity** is a primary doorway.
- The three doorways must remain distinct but visibly connected.
- **DealVault** is a shared trust, agreement, milestone, proof, and accountability layer that supports work across VestBlock. It is not the master brand and not a detached software company.
- The Brain and Command Center are supporting intelligence and execution capabilities. They help VestBlock learn, prioritize, and act; they do not replace the public Capital, Deals, and Opportunity structure.

### Scope

- Real estate is an important VestBlock vertical, not the definition of the company.
- Business capital, real-estate capital, deals, partners, services, education, resources, growth, DealVault, strategy, and future qualified opportunities can coexist under the VestBlock roof.
- Existing useful routes and capabilities should be connected to the master structure, not erased simply because they are not featured in the hero.
- A focused fix or addition must not change the company position, audience, information hierarchy, visual world, or wording strategy unless the owner explicitly approves that change.

### Visual identity

- Preserve the approved graphite, ivory, and electric-lime color world.
- Preserve the approved VB mark and VestBlock name.
- Use the mark as an anchored brand identifier, not as a floating object, decorative stamp, spinning primitive, or substitute for a visual concept.
- The experience should feel authored, editorial, financially literate, tactile, and precise.
- Premium quality does not mean adding more glow, 3D objects, glass cards, gradients, or effects.

## Public positioning

### Approved master explanation

> VestBlock brings capital access, deal pathways, and opportunities to build, acquire, or grow into one coordinated place. Understand your options, prepare what matters, and act with a clear next step.

This is the proposed Phase 1 explanation for approval. It preserves the original consolidation decision without promising funding, matches, returns, approvals, closings, or timelines.

### Five-second comprehension test

A new visitor should understand that:

1. VestBlock helps people make a next move.
2. The platform brings together Capital, Deals, and Opportunity.
3. VestBlock helps the visitor understand options, prepare useful information, and take an appropriate next step.
4. Real estate is present where relevant, but VestBlock is not limited to it.
5. The immediate action is to tell VestBlock what the visitor is working toward.

## Phrase ledger

### Phrases that must remain available

- **VestBlock**
- **VestBlock LLC** where the legal name is appropriate
- **Find your next move.**
- **Capital**
- **Deals**
- **Opportunity**
- **DealVault**
- **Capital + Deals + Opportunity** as an organizing statement

### Phrases that may change with editorial review

- Supporting hero explanation
- CTA wording
- Doorway descriptions
- Section headings
- Navigation microcopy
- Metadata descriptions
- Vertical-specific explanations
- Educational and resource copy

Changes to these phrases must still pass the immutable brand decisions and claim guardrails.

### Phrases that require explicit approval before public use

- Any new master-brand category replacing Capital, Deals, or Opportunity
- Any new tagline replacing **Find your next move.**
- Any statement that VestBlock is primarily or exclusively a real-estate platform
- Any statement that VestBlock is primarily or exclusively a lender marketplace, software product, DealVault product, partner network, lead-generation service, or agency
- Any homepage position that elevates one vertical into the company definition
- Any new visual metaphor that changes how the company is understood
- Any structural removal of a primary doorway or supporting trust/intelligence layer

### Customer-facing phrases to remove or avoid

Unless the term is unavoidable and explained in plain language, do not expose:

- machinery
- engine
- workflow
- routing layer
- orchestration surface
- operating layer
- agent pipeline
- lane
- operator
- interface
- rebrand
- vibe-coded technical language
- unexplained AEO, GEO, or internal acronyms

Use language that names what the visitor can understand, prepare, compare, explore, or do next.

## Claim guardrails

- Do not guarantee funding, approval, rates, rankings, matches, deals, closings, returns, revenue, or timelines.
- Do not describe a referral or partner introduction as independent financial, legal, tax, or investment advice.
- Separate available capabilities from future capabilities.
- Use **may**, **can help**, **when appropriate**, or **subject to review** when the result depends on third parties or eligibility.
- Preserve privacy and consent boundaries in outreach and data collection.

## Phase 1 visual direction for approval

Working art-direction name: **Material Ledger**.

Material Ledger translates the existing Capital, Deals, and Opportunity structure into a recognizable next-move workspace. A person compares capital-readiness materials, an active deal brief, and opportunities to build, acquire, or grow. A subtle sources-and-uses reference provides financial depth without turning the public page into a chart or financial diagram.

The system uses scale, crop, light, paper, graphite, and restrained signal lime. Type is part of the composition rather than an overlay on a stock background. The three primary doorways appear as one connected decision in progress, not three equal cards. Capital materials sit beside a deal dossier and opportunity folio, while a lime-tabbed DealVault record holds the active materials together underneath.

The VB mark is fixed into the masthead and reappears only when a transition structurally reveals it. It never floats. DealVault appears later as the durable record beneath an active decision, while the Brain and Command Center appear as the quiet intelligence coordinating the next action.

The direction is cinematic because the decision advances through scroll, not because decorative objects move. The eventual sequence should use camera crop, focus, hand and document choreography, typographic masks, and material transitions. Motion must remain subordinate to comprehension and must include a complete reduced-motion state.
